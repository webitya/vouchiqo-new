2. TYPOGRAPHY SYSTEM & HIERARCHY SPECIFICATION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The typography pairs a clean humanist geometric sans for supreme reading clarity
with a bold, punchy, editorial geometric sans for brand identity.

A. PRIMARY FONT: Plus Jakarta Sans
   Family: 'Plus Jakarta Sans', sans-serif
   Import: @import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Plus+Jakarta+Sans:ital,wght@0,200..800;1,200..800&display=swap');
   Role: Universal Body, UI Controls, Form Elements, and Navigation
   Where to use:
   • Body copy, paragraphs, deal terms and conditions, FAQ answers
   • Navbar links, footer links, dropdown item text, mobile bottom nav labels
   • Form inputs, textareas, placeholder copy, labels, validation messages
   • Table cells, descriptions, user profile info, dates and timestamps
   • Standard button labels (Search, Cancel, Filter, Load More)

   Weights & Usage:
   - 400 (Regular): Standard body text, fine print, descriptions
   - 500 (Medium): Form labels, navigation links, secondary metadata
   - 600 (Semi-Bold): Form inputs, interactive action labels, filter chips
   - 700 (Bold): Emphasized numbers, strong body callouts

B. SECONDARY FONT: Montserrat
   Family: 'Montserrat', sans-serif
   Import: @import url('https://fonts.googleapis.com/css2?family=Montserrat:ital,wght@0,100..900;1,100..900&display=swap');
   Role: Headings, Display Headlines, Numerals, Discount Callouts & Badges
   Where to use:
   • Page titles (h1) and Section titles (h2, h3, h4)
   • Hero marketing headlines and promotional taglines
   • Discount percentages (e.g., "50% OFF", "FLAT ₹200 CASHBACK")
   • Coupon codes (e.g., "VOUCH50", "DIWALI2026")
   • Kicker badges (e.g., "TRENDING NOW", "DEALS OF THE DAY", "VERIFIED STORE")
   • Big metric numbers on KPI cards and dashboard stats
   • CTA button text for high-impact buttons ("GRAB OFFER", "CLAIM NOW")

   Weights & Usage:
   - 600 (Semi-Bold): Category card titles, filter section headers
   - 700 (Bold): Main section titles, card discount values, badges
   - 800 (Extra-Bold): Hero headers, big discount callouts, coupon codes
